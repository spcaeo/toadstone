Server logs

A log says what search engines actually fetched, which no crawl can tell you. Upload one and it is compared against a crawl: pages Google asks for that nothing links to, pages you crawled that no crawler has ever requested, and budget spent on URLs that only ever error.

The file is read, analysed and discarded. It is never written to disk and never stored with the crawl. An access log is a list of who visited your site and from where, and it belongs to you.
